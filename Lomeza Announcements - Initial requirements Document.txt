# Lomeza Announcements
## Initial Product Requirements Document

**Project:** Lomeza Announcements  
**Status:** Initial requirements draft  
**Platform:** Internal company communications platform

---

## 1. Project Overview

Lomeza Announcements will serve as a central internal communications platform for Lomeza Mining Services.

Its purpose is to provide employees with a reliable way to receive company information, management announcements, operational updates, safety communications, events and important company documents.

The platform will support multiple communication channels, including:

- Web-based employee portal.
- Email notifications.
- Digital noticeboards displayed on workplace screens.

The system should accommodate employees across different company sites, departments and organisational roles.

The platform should be designed to support future expansion without requiring major changes to its underlying architecture.

## 2. Supported Content Types

The platform must support:

| Content type | Description |
|---|---|
| Company announcements | General company communications and updates. |
| Management notices | Official communications from company management. |
| HR communications | Employee-related information, policies and notices. |
| Safety communications | Workplace safety information and notices. |
| Operational updates | Site-specific and company-wide operational information. |
| Company events | Events, meetings, celebrations and scheduled activities. |
| Company documents | Policies, procedures, forms and downloadable resources. |
| Toolbox talks | Safety briefings, supporting material and attendance records. |

Content should support appropriate formatting and attachments, including images, PDFs and other relevant documents.

## 3. User Roles and Publishing Permissions

Management and authorised administrators will be permitted to publish announcements.

The platform must support permission-based publishing.

### Proposed roles

- Super Administrator
- Administrator
- Management
- Communications
- Site Management
- Supervisor
- Employee

These are proposed roles and will require confirmation.

### Publishing permissions

The platform must support two publishing workflows:

**Direct publishing**

Authorised users with permission to publish independently can publish announcements without requiring additional approval.

**Approval-based publishing**

Authors without direct publishing permission must submit announcements for approval before publication.

The approval requirement must be determined by the author's permissions rather than assuming that every announcement requires approval.

The platform must retain information about who created, approved and published each announcement.

## 4. Announcement Configuration

Each announcement should have configurable options controlled by its author, subject to their permissions.

### Content settings

- Announcement title.
- Announcement body.
- Category.
- Priority.
- Featured status.
- Attachments.
- Publication date and time.
- Expiration date and time.
- Target audience.

### Distribution settings

Authors should be able to select the applicable distribution channels:

- Employee web portal.
- Email notifications.
- Digital noticeboards.

An announcement should not automatically be distributed through every channel unless the author selects those channels or an applicable organisational default is configured.

### Interaction settings

Authors should be able to enable or disable the following features independently for each announcement:

| Feature | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement tracking | Records when an employee opens the announcement. |
| Comments | Allows employees to comment on the announcement. |
| Reactions | Allows employees to react to the announcement. |

These settings should be configurable when creating or editing an announcement.

The platform must respect the author's permissions and any organisational restrictions.

## 5. Automatic Acknowledgement and Read Receipts

Acknowledgement tracking must function as an automatic read receipt.

Employees must not be required to click a separate "Acknowledged" button.

### Expected behaviour

1. An employee receives an announcement.
2. The employee opens the announcement.
3. The system automatically records the acknowledgement.
4. The system stores the employee's identity and the date and time of the first acknowledgement.
5. The announcement author or an authorised administrator can view acknowledgement statistics.

### Important implementation rules

- Opening the announcement detail should trigger acknowledgement.
- Merely displaying an announcement in the feed must not trigger acknowledgement.
- Each employee should have one acknowledgement record per announcement.
- Reopening an announcement must not create duplicate acknowledgement records.
- The original acknowledgement timestamp should be preserved.
- Acknowledgement tracking must be optional per announcement.

### Reporting

Authorised users should be able to view:

- Total employees targeted.
- Number of employees who have opened the announcement.
- Number of employees who have not opened it.
- Acknowledgement percentage.
- Individual acknowledgement records and timestamps.

The system should distinguish between an announcement being delivered, opened and acknowledged where channel-specific delivery information is available.

**Note:** An automatic acknowledgement confirms that the announcement was opened. It does not prove that the employee read or understood every word.

## 6. Employee Interaction

Employees should be able to interact with announcements when the author enables the applicable features.

### Comments

- Employees can submit comments.
- Comments should identify their author and creation timestamp.
- Authors and authorised administrators should be able to moderate comments.
- Comments should be associated with their original announcement.

### Reactions

- Employees can react to announcements.
- The system should prevent duplicate identical reactions from the same employee.
- Reaction counts should be visible where reactions are enabled.

### Acknowledgements

- Automatically recorded when an employee opens an announcement.
- No separate acknowledgement button.
- Visible to authorised users through reporting.

## 7. Employee Accounts and Authentication

Employee accounts must be linked to existing Lomeza company email addresses.

The platform must not operate as an unrestricted public registration service.

### Proposed account creation process

1. An authorised administrator creates or imports an employee profile.
2. The employee's existing company email address is associated with the profile.
3. An invitation is sent to that address.
4. The employee verifies ownership of the email address.
5. The employee completes account setup.
6. The account is linked to the employee profile.

Supabase Auth will manage authentication.

Employee profiles will contain relevant organisational information, including:

- Employee name.
- Company email address.
- Employee number, where applicable.
- Assigned site.
- Assigned department.
- Assigned platform role.
- Account status.

## 8. Organisation Structure and Audience Targeting

The platform must support communication across multiple company sites and departments.

Proposed initial sites:

- Head Office.
- Kalagadi.
- Sundra.
- Devon.

Announcements must support targeting based on:

- Entire company.
- Individual sites.
- Departments.
- Employee roles.
- Combinations of the above.

The system should ensure that employees only receive announcements intended for their assigned audience.

## 9. Digital Noticeboards

The platform must support displaying selected announcements on workplace screens.

Digital noticeboards should have a dedicated display interface.

Proposed functionality:

- Display published announcements.
- Display images and posters.
- Rotate through selected announcements.
- Support scheduled content.
- Allow authorised administrators to manage screens and playlists.
- Restrict the display interface from accessing administrative functionality.

## 10. Company Documents and Events

The platform must support company documents and event-related communications.

Documents should support categorisation, attachments and appropriate access restrictions.

Events should support:

- Event title and description.
- Date and time.
- Location.
- Target audience.
- Supporting attachments.
- Attendance tracking where required.

Toolbox talks should support safety-related content and attendance records.

## 11. Administration, Security and Auditability

The platform must implement role-based permissions and database-level access restrictions.

Security requirements:

- Supabase Row Level Security.
- Restricted employee registration.
- Controlled publishing permissions.
- Protected administrative functionality.
- Restricted access to confidential documents.
- Secure attachment storage.
- Site and department-based access controls.

The system must maintain audit records for important actions, including:

- Announcement creation.
- Announcement modification.
- Approval decisions.
- Publication and archival.
- Changes to employee permissions.
- Administrative changes.

## 12. Proposed Initial Development Scope

The initial implementation should focus on:

1. Supabase authentication.
2. Employee profiles.
3. Sites and departments.
4. Roles and permissions.
5. Announcement categories.
6. Announcement creation and management.
7. Audience targeting.
8. Approval and publishing workflows.
9. Email and portal distribution.
10. Automatic acknowledgement tracking.
11. Comments and reactions.
12. Basic administration and audit logging.

Digital noticeboards, advanced event management and toolbox talk management can be implemented as subsequent modules, while ensuring the initial architecture accommodates them.

## 13. Outstanding Management Decisions

The following matters require confirmation before the final database schema and detailed implementation are approved:

- Final employee roles and permission definitions.
- Whether roles are global, site-specific or both.
- Whether departments are shared across sites.
- Which announcement categories require approval.
- Whether confidential HR communications are required.
- Which employees may create and manage digital noticeboards.
- Whether employees should receive email notifications for every announcement or only selected categories and priorities.
- Whether comment moderation is required.
- Whether employees without individual accounts will access information exclusively through physical or digital noticeboards.
- Whether event attendance and toolbox talk attendance require formal reporting.
